## How each image was checked

The "Verification" column says exactly what was checked. Photographer names and
page URLs come from the Unsplash search/listing pages; for rows marked "photo
page not opened" the license was not read on the photo page itself — only that
Unsplash listed the photo as free (not Unsplash+). Every image was looked at
before use to confirm it matches the product type; the accuracy limits are
listed below.
